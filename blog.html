Understanding Smart Contracts and Their Role in NFTs

Smart contracts are the backbone of NFT technology. These self-executing contracts allow transactions without intermediaries, ensuring transparency and security. Whether it’s an artist setting royalties or a game developer issuing rare items, smart contracts make it possible to automate and protect digital value.
